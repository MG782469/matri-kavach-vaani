How it works

  1. Say Hi, choose a language, then choose the emergency from the same list Matri-Kavach uses. You choose it; Vaani never diagnoses.
  2. Patient details by voice: say everything at once, or let Vaani ask one by one. Gnani Prisma transcribes each answer.
  3. Missing or conflicting details? Vaani asks with Gnani Timbre, and your answer changes only that field.
  4. Timbre reads every digit back. Say “हाँ, सब सही है” to approve this exact version.
  5. Send a simulated referral. The Matri-Kavach demo case opens with your chosen emergency, and no patient details are sent.
